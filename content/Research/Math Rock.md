### TL;DR
This paper analyzes rhythms in math rock/emo bands using the discrete #fourierTransform (DFT). Rhythms are encoded as weighted onsets based on Lerdahl and Jackendoff preference rules. I show the DFT is able to capture different layers of the meter (if it is identified in the weighting).
