## Reshaping Rhythm: An Analysis of Prokofiev's Piano Sonata No. 4

### Format (When): Where
* Presentation (2021): European Music Analysis Conference, Moscow (online)

### TL;DR
Prokofiev's 4th piano sonata uses significant use of rhythmic displacement and imitation. This paper uses the discrete #fourierTransform for rhythmic analysis of the sonata, showing that the DFT captures these moments and, further, shows aspects of the form.
