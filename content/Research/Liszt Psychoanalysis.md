### Presentation Title
 A Message from the Real: Paradigmatic and Syntagmatic Quilting in Liszt's _Liebestraum No. 1_

### Format/Where/When
* Presentation: Fourth Biennial LSU Music Colloquium, 2017

### Summary
Liszt wrote a handful of songs and arranged them for piano. In *Hohe Liebe* (arranged as *Liebestraum No. 2*) there are jarring changes between the two pieces. I interpret these changes to have a significant interpretive impact.
