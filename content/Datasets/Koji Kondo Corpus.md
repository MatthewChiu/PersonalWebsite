## Background
"Let's a go!" Much of the music in the Mario and Legend of Zelda franchises are written by Koji Kondo. But yet, they sound so unique to each game _and_ within the franchise. We built a dataset and studied similarities and differences between their harmonic language. 

We presented this talk at the National Association for Video Game Music (NAfVGM) and the Informatics Interest Group at #SMT. Here's a video of the talk [of the talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=agFlat6AG58).
